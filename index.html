The festival

BKKIFF returns 13–27 September 2026 with 150+ films, a film market, workshops and talks. Actors Ananda Everingham and Aim Bhumibhat Thavornsiri are this year's ambassadors.

Two retrospectives

Seven films by Palme d'Or winner Shōhei Imamura at Paragon, and Thai classics at Doc Club & Friends and House Samyan – part of a tribute to producer Visute Poolvoralaks.
